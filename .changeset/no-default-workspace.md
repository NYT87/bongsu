Nothing lands in a workspace nobody chose. A draft from a store's inbox goes to the workspace whose plan has its
project, else to the workspace the store's new **Inbox feeds** setting names (dashboard → Media); without one, or
for an archived workspace, it waits. An upgraded instance that keeps `default` has its stores feed it, as before.
A broken media store is told to the instance admins (the audit log, and an email with a Mailer) instead of the default
workspace, and the app tells someone signed in without a workspace so, on UTC.
