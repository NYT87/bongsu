A post that went through a dry run publishes for real once it's approved again. A Trial Reel copy goes out only when the approval covered it: turning the switch on afterwards asks for a new approval. A name another workspace already used (a post it recorded, or a job it left) can't be planned again: you're asked to pick another, instead of the post being skipped without a word.
