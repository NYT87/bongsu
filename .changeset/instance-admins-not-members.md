Instance admins no longer appear among a workspace's members, nor in its member count, and they don't count toward "a workspace keeps one admin": every workspace keeps an admin of its own, and an instance admin's own membership can always be removed. They're managed from a new **Instance admins** card on the admin dashboard's Workspaces page (remove one, or make anyone on the instance one); Members keeps **Make instance admin**.
