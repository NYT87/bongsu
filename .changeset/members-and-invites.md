Members and invites: a workspace's admins invite people by email with a role (a one-time link for 7 days, always
shown to copy and emailed when a mailer is set), change roles and remove members from the dashboard's Members page.
Admins and editors join in the dashboard, creators in the app, with a new passkey or the one they have. A removed
member is cut off at once, and a workspace always keeps one admin. A new workspace's first admin can be invited by
email.
