Privacy policy

This site runs Bongsu, open-source software that publishes posts and videos on the dates its owners plan, once a person has approved each one. It's run by its owner, not by the Bongsu project: questions about this instance go to the person who gave you access to it.

What it keeps

  • The plan: posts and videos, their captions, titles and comments, when they go out, and who approved them.
  • The accounts you connect (Instagram, YouTube, Threads and the like) and the access their sign-in grants, stored encrypted in this instance's database.
  • Your name, email, passkeys and the devices that receive its notifications.
  • Results of what it published: views, likes, comments and followers, as the platforms report them.

The media itself stays where you keep it (your Google Drive or a storage bucket); the database only refers to it.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, this instance asks for:

  • Google Drive: to read the files in its workspaces' folders, send them to the platforms you publish to, make the folders it needs, take uploads from its app, and move published files to a _posted folder. It never deletes or trashes a file.
  • YouTube: to upload the videos you approved, set their details, subtitles and thumbnails, make them public at the planned time, and post, read and answer comments on your channel's videos.
  • YouTube Analytics, read only: the subscribers each video brought.

This data is used only to provide these features to you. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone except the platforms you publish to and the tools this instance's admins connect with their own keys. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Remove a connection or a storage from the dashboard, or withdraw this instance's access in your Google account (Third-party connections) or in the platform's own settings.
  • Ask the person who runs this instance to export or delete what it keeps about you.

YouTube data goes through the YouTube API Services: Google's own handling of it is in the Google Privacy Policy. The rules for using this instance are in its terms of service.
